Privacy Policy for CityPulse AI

Last updated: June 03, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the CityPulse AI mobile application ("the App") and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

We use your personal data to provide and improve the App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Approximate Location Data: We collect information about your approximate location to provide city-specific news, local weather, and regional intelligence.
  • Usage Data: Collected automatically when using the App.

Usage Data

Usage Data may include information such as your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, the pages of our App that you visit, the time and date of your visit, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

2. Third-Party Services & AI Integration

To provide our autonomous intelligence services, we utilize several third-party providers who may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services: For location detection and core app functionality.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: To monitor and analyze the use of our App.
  • Google Gemini AI: Used for generating neural news summaries based on headlines.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing secure in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • NewsAPI.org: As our primary data source for global news headlines.

3. How We Use Your Information

CityPulse AI uses the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our App: Including monitoring usage and stability.
  • To provide localized intelligence: Your location is used solely to identify your city for news and weather delivery.
  • To manage your subscription: To verify your "Pulse Elite" status via Google Play Billing.
  • To improve our service: Utilizing anonymized analytics to understand feature engagement.

4. Data Retention and Security

We will retain your information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We value your trust in providing us your information, and we use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.

6. Your Rights

Since CityPulse AI does not require a user account, most preferences (like pinned locations and language settings) are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the App's cache/data or by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
