This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the CityPulse AI mobile application ("the App").

1. Information Collection and Use

We use your personal data to provide and improve the App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Types of Data Collected

  • Approximate Location Data: We collect approximate location data (city-level) to deliver localized news, weather, and regional intelligence. This data is not shared with third parties for advertising.
  • Usage Data & Identifiers: We collect standard diagnostic data and device identifiers to monitor app stability and analyze feature engagement.

2. Third-Party Services & News Policy

CityPulse AI is a news aggregation platform. To provide our services, we utilize the following third-party providers:

  • NewsAPI.org: Our primary source for real-time news headlines. Every article includes the original publisher's name and a direct link to the original source for authenticity and verification.
  • Google Gemini AI: Used to generate concise neural summaries based on publicly available news headlines.
  • Google Play Services & Billing: For location detection, app functionality, and secure subscription management.
  • Firebase Analytics: To monitor app performance and user trends.

3. Content Authenticity

In compliance with the Google Play News and Magazines policy, CityPulse AI ensures that all news content is:

  • Attributed to the original publisher or author.
  • Linked directly to the original source website.
  • Updated in real-time to ensure content is fresh and relevant.
